## Color example sources

- [CHERRY MX Green comparison table](https://www.cherry.de/en-us/product/mx-green) lists Red as linear, Brown as tactile, Blue as clicky, and also Black as linear, Purple as tactile, Green as clicky.
- [CHERRY MX Black](https://www.cherry.de/en-gb/product/mx2a-black) and [CHERRY MX Purple](https://www.cherry.de/en-gb/product/mx-purple) confirm the alternate stem colors/types.
- The drawings are original generic switch illustrations. They do not reproduce CHERRY product imagery or imply those exact models are depicted.
