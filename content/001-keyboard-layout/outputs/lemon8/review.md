## What to repeat / change

### Repeat

- Keep canonical research separate from platform adaptation. The existing sourced
  keyboard narrative adapted into eight cards without rewriting the factual core.
- Use one decision per card and finish with a compact workflow-based recommendation.
- Keep a reproducible HTML-to-PNG renderer so visual changes can be applied across
  the full carousel consistently.
- Prefer realistic, brandless layout renders for category education when a real
  product photograph would imply a specific model.

### Change next time

- Decide the cover headline and caption earlier; both materially affect the visual
  hierarchy and should be part of `/adapt`, not a late packaging pass.
- Preview with realistic imagery before approving the first visual direction.
- Capture the final post URL, account, exact publish time, final headline, and
  caption immediately after upload.
- Complete and record the source-freshness check before publishing rather than
  leaving it as an unresolved package field.
- Add a compact final-publish checklist that reflects the actual uploaded version,
  since the working package changed after initial QA.
