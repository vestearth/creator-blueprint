# Tech Channel Audience

## Primary audience

Thai-speaking developers, knowledge workers, and technology enthusiasts who want
practical explanations rather than news recaps or promotional summaries.

## Viewer needs

- Understand unfamiliar technology without reading many fragmented sources
- Compare options before spending time or money
- Learn how tools behave in real workflows
- Get a clear recommendation with its trade-offs

## Non-goals

- Chasing every AI news cycle
- Serving advanced specialists at the expense of a clear introduction
- Publishing generic listicles with no tested or reasoned point of view
