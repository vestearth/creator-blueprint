# Tech Channel Identity

## Positioning

Technology explained by someone who actually uses it.

The channel turns practical experience with AI, software development, and
mechanical keyboards into clear, useful explanations. AI may assist production,
but the channel's judgment, structure, and conclusions must remain identifiable.

## Viewer promise

Every post should help the viewer understand a technology choice, use a tool
more effectively, or avoid a practical mistake.

## Voice

- Clear, grounded, and curious
- Technically credible without unnecessary jargon
- Honest about uncertainty and trade-offs
- No exaggerated certainty, manufactured urgency, or empty hype

## Audience relationship

- Use `เรา` for both narrator and audience to create a shared exploration.
- Avoid `ผม` and `คุณ` in narration unless a quotation or context requires them.
- Prefer inclusive phrasing such as `ถ้าเราใช้...` and `ลองดูว่าเรา...`.

## Format direction

- Primary v0: evergreen Lemon8 carousel posts built from verified canonical content
- Presentation: diagram-led and mobile-readable, with real screenshots or product
  photos when factual detail requires them
- Future adapters: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, blog, and podcast; these do
  not determine the current core architecture
