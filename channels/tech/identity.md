# Tech Channel Identity

## Positioning

Technology explained by someone who actually uses it.

The channel turns practical experience with AI, software development, and
mechanical keyboards into clear, useful explanations. AI may assist production,
but the channel's judgment, structure, and conclusions must remain identifiable.

## Viewer promise

Every video should help the viewer understand a technology choice, use a tool
more effectively, or avoid a practical mistake.

## Voice

- Clear, grounded, and curious
- Technically credible without unnecessary jargon
- Honest about uncertainty and trade-offs
- No exaggerated certainty, manufactured urgency, or empty hype

## Format direction

- Primary: evergreen YouTube videos of approximately 6–12 minutes
- Secondary: Shorts derived from strong moments in published long-form videos
- Presentation: faceless is acceptable when narration and visuals add real value
