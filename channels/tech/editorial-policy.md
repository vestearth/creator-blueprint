# Tech Channel Editorial Policy

## Originality

Each content item must contain a recognizable editorial contribution: a useful model,
comparison, demonstration, conclusion, or experience-based interpretation.
Changing a topic inside a repeated template is not sufficient.

## Research and claims

- Record the source, access date, and supported claim in `research.md`.
- Prefer primary sources for product behavior, policies, and specifications.
- Mark uncertain or experience-based statements explicitly.
- Recheck time-sensitive claims near publication.

## AI use

AI may assist research, outlining, drafting, narration, translation, visuals, and
editing. A human remains responsible for the angle, factual accuracy, rights,
and final editorial decision.

Evaluate platform disclosure requirements for realistic synthetic or altered
content before publishing. Disclosure is a publishing decision, not a substitute
for accuracy or consent.

## Rights and attribution

- Record the source and usage basis of each non-original asset.
- Do not assume commentary, attribution, or AI transformation grants permission.
- Avoid cloned voices or realistic depictions of real people without a valid basis.
- Replace any asset whose usage basis cannot be explained during final review.

## Packaging integrity

The title, cover, and caption may create curiosity but must represent the outcome
the content actually delivers.
