# Pre-publish QA

## Editorial

- [ ] The video fulfills the brief's viewer promise.
- [ ] The channel contributes a clear original explanation or point of view.
- [ ] The opening gives the viewer a reason to continue.
- [ ] Repetition and unsupported filler have been removed.

## Accuracy

- [ ] Material factual claims map to `research.md`.
- [ ] Time-sensitive claims were rechecked near publication.
- [ ] Opinion, inference, and uncertainty are presented honestly.

## Rights and safety

- [ ] Every non-original visual and audio asset has a recorded usage basis.
- [ ] Names, brands, voices, and likenesses are used responsibly.
- [ ] Platform AI/synthetic-content disclosure was evaluated.
- [ ] No unresolved copyright or privacy concern remains.

## Packaging and playback

- [ ] Title and thumbnail match the delivered outcome.
- [ ] Captions, names, numbers, and on-screen text were checked.
- [ ] Audio is intelligible and levels are consistent.
- [ ] Visuals are readable on a phone-sized screen.
- [ ] Description, chapters, links, and credits are ready.
- [ ] The final export was watched from beginning to end.
